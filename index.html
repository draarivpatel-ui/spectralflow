Method notes

BGK collision

Populations relax toward the discrete Maxwellian at rate omega = 1 / tau, with tau = 3 nu + 1/2. Pressure follows the ideal lattice gas law p = rho / 3.

fi(x + ci, t + 1) = fi(x, t) + ω (fieq − fi)

Boundaries

The west inlet uses Zou-He velocity closure, the east outlet copies outgoing populations, and halfway bounce-back reflects any population that would stream out of a solid node. The reflected momentum gives the drag and lift proxies directly.

F = ∑ 2 ci fi*

NACA 4 digit

Thickness uses the closed trailing edge polynomial with the -0.1036 coefficient. Camber is a pair of parabolas meeting at the maximum camber position, and thickness is offset along the local normal.

yt = 5t (0.2969√x − 0.1260x − 0.3516x2 + 0.2843x3 − 0.1036x4)
