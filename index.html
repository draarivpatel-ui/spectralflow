Appendix A. Governing Lattice Boltzmann & Aerodynamic Formulation

A.1 Single-Relaxation BGK Collision

Nine discrete particle populations relax toward the second-order truncated Maxwellian equilibrium at rate ω = 1 / τ, where kinematic viscosity is ν = cs2(τ − 1/2) and cs2 = 1/3. As τ → 0.5, dispersive pre-shock oscillations grow rapidly unless high-Mach nodes are regularized.

fi(x + ciΔt, t + Δt) = fi(x, t) + ω [fieq(ρ, u) − fi(x, t)]

A.2 Zou-He Inlet & Bounce-Back Links

The west boundary reconstructs density and inward populations from prescribed inflow velocity u0. Solid obstacle nodes reflect incoming populations halfway along each cut lattice link, enforcing no-slip walls and yielding hydrodynamic force directly by momentum exchange.

F = ∑links 2 ci fi*(xf, t),   Cp = (ρ − ρ0) / (3 · ½ρ0u02)

A.3 NACA 4-Digit Normal Offset

Half-thickness uses the closed trailing-edge polynomial (−0.1036 x4) and is offset perpendicular to the parabolic mean camber line yc(x) at local angle θ = atan(dyc/dx) across cosine-clustered stations.

yt = 5t (0.2969√x − 0.1260x − 0.3516x2 + 0.2843x3 − 0.1036x4)
